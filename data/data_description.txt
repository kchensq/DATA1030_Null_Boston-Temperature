time: Calendar date of the observation (local time, format YYYY-MM-DD)

temperature_2m_mean: Daily mean air temperature at 2 m above ground, in degrees Celsius

precipitation_sum: Total daily precipitation, including rain, showers and snowfall, in millimeters

wind_speed_10m_max: Maximum wind speed at 10 m above ground on a day, in kilometers per hour

snowfall_sum: Total daily snowfall, in centimeters
